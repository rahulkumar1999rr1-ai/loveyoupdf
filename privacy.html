Privacy notes

In this starter project, PDF and image processing is designed to run in the browser; files are not intentionally uploaded to a MyPDF backend. The OCR and DOCX features load open-source libraries and language data from third-party CDNs, which may receive standard network metadata.

Do not process confidential, medical, financial or legally sensitive documents with an unreviewed demo. Your hosting provider may collect technical logs. If you add analytics, advertising, accounts, or server processing, update this policy to accurately explain those services.

Before public launch, replace placeholders and have the policy reviewed for your intended use.
